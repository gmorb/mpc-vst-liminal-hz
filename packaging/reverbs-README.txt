Put your reverb impulse responses (.wav files) in the "Liminal Hz" folder at the top of this drive (Liminal Hz/reverbs/),
or in any folder named "reverbs" on your memory card or the Force's internal drive. Not here: updates replace the
plugin's folder (Liminal Hz moves IRs it finds here into Liminal Hz/reverbs/ the first time it starts). Subfolders
become packs. After adding files, press RESCAN.

Mono or stereo; 16/24/32-bit PCM or 32/64-bit float (also the "extensible" WAVs many DAWs write); any sample rate.
IRs up to 5 seconds are used in full (20 seconds with LONG IR on); longer ones are cut there (faded out). END shortens them further.
BROWSE TONE3000 downloads reverb and space IRs into Liminal Hz/reverbs/TONE3000/.
The plugin remembers where your reverbs folders are (a small hidden file, .reverbs-index, next to the plugin), so it starts
fast. If you add a new reverbs folder on another card, press RESCAN once and it is found and remembered.
