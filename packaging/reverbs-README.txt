Put reverb impulse responses (.wav files) here, or in any folder named "reverbs" on your memory card or the Force's
internal drive. Subfolders become packs. After adding files, press RESCAN.

Mono or stereo; 16/24/32-bit PCM or 32/64-bit float (also the "extensible" WAVs many DAWs write); any sample rate.
IRs up to 5 seconds are used in full; longer ones are cut to 5 s (faded out). LENGTH shortens them further.
BROWSE TONE3000 downloads reverb and space IRs into reverbs/TONE3000/.
